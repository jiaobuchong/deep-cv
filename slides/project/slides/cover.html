Prototype-initialized adaptation of ViT-B/16 for 10,000-class fine-grained classification

Full fine-tuning reaches 76.22% and LoRA 71.90% top-1 on CodaBench val, inside a 2-hour, 48 GB training budget

[Member names] · 7 November 2026

Good afternoon. We are Team 06. Our task was to adapt one pretrained Vision Transformer, ViT-B/16, to a 10,000-class fine-grained dataset under a fixed compute budget, with both full fine-tuning and parameter-efficient methods. The headline: our final full fine-tuning model reaches 76.22% top-1 on the CodaBench validation set, and our LoRA model reaches 71.90% while training only 1.375% of the backbone. The single change that mattered most was how we initialize the 10,000-class classifier head.
