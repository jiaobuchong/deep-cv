Our method

Our pipeline: prototype head, fine-tune, flip TTA

1 · Extract features

Run the frozen ViT-B/16 once over all training images and keep the 768-d CLS features.

2 · Prototype head

Class weight = L2-normalized mean feature of the class × temperature 40. No gradient steps.

3 · Fine-tune

Full fine-tuning, or LoRA r = 8 on qkv, proj, fc1 and fc2 (1.375% of the backbone).

4 · Predict

Average the logits of each image and its horizontal flip (test-time augmentation).

Every run is budget-aware: after 120 steps the measured speed sets how many steps fit in 1.2 GPU-hours, and the cosine learning-rate schedule is compressed to fit.

Here is the whole pipeline. Step one: we run the frozen backbone once over all training images and keep the 768-dimensional CLS features. Step two, the key design choice: each class's weight in the new 10,000-class head is the L2-normalized mean of its features, scaled by a temperature of 40. That takes no gradient steps at all. Step three: we fine-tune, either the whole network or LoRA with rank 8 on every linear layer of every block, which is 1.375% of the backbone. Step four: at prediction time we average the logits of the image and its horizontal flip. Every run is also budget-aware. After 120 steps we measure the speed, compute how many steps fit into 1.2 GPU-hours, and compress the cosine schedule if needed, so no run can exceed the budget.
