The new head has 10,000 classes and 7.69 million parameters, so how we start it matters. We compared three options. Random: the timm default, small random weights, so the head knows nothing at the start. Prototype, our choice: each class weight is the normalized mean feature of that class, times a temperature. It needs no training and already gives 44% before any fine-tuning. Linear probe: we start from the prototypes and train only the head for 10 epochs on frozen features. It starts strongest, at 62%. This is the classic linear-probe-then-fine-tune recipe, and it was our first design. Intuition says the strongest starting head should win. The next slide shows it does not.
