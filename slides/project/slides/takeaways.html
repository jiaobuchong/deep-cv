To sum up in four points. One: initialize the 10,000-class head from class prototypes, not from a linear probe; that alone is worth 2.5 to 3.1 points. Two: for LoRA, add capacity in the MLP rather than tuning the learning rate. Three: full fine-tuning should use the whole budget, while LoRA should stop at about 3 epochs. And four, the outcome: 76.22% with full fine-tuning and 71.90% with LoRA on CodaBench val, both well inside the compute budget.
