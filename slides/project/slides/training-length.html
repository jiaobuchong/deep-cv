Third finding: training length behaves in opposite ways for the two methods. Full fine-tuning keeps improving: 3 to 8 epochs adds 1.4 points with the linear-probe head and 2.2 points on CodaBench with the prototype head, which is how we got to 76.22%. LoRA saturates after about 3 epochs. With the qkv-only LoRA, 10 epochs is no better than 3; with the prototype head, 6 epochs is even 0.46 points worse than 3. Our reading: full fine-tuning uses a small learning rate, 1e-4, with layer-wise decay, so it moves gently; LoRA uses 1e-3 and starts to memorize the training images. So we train full fine-tuning for the whole budget and stop LoRA at 3 epochs.
