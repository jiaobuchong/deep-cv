Before trying anything new, we reproduced the three reference numbers from the brief. The zero-shot prototype head gives 43.95% against 43.7%, full fine-tuning for 3 epochs gives 70.88% against 70.8%, and LoRA with rank 8 on the attention qkv projection gives 68.57% against 68.3%. So our pipeline matches the reference. The validation labels are hidden, so we held out 2 training images per class, 20,000 images, as a local validation set. Holdout and CodaBench agree within 0.3 points on the two models we checked, so the ablations that follow are on holdout.
