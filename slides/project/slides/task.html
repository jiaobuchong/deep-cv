The dataset has 10,000 fine-grained classes but only 45 training images per class, 450,000 images in total. So per class this is close to a few-shot problem. The backbone is fixed: ViT-B/16, pretrained on ImageNet-21k and fine-tuned on ImageNet-1k. Every training run must fit in 2 hours on 48 GB of GPU memory. We trained on one A100 with 80 GB, so we converted the budget to 96 GB-hours divided by 80 GB, which is 1.2 hours per run, counting head initialization, training and per-epoch evaluation. The parameter-efficient track should train less than 5% of the parameters. We report all four metrics the brief asks for: top-1 accuracy, trainable parameters, inference latency and training GPU-hours.
