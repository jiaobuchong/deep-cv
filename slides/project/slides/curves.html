Here are the learning curves behind that finding. The leftmost point is the head on its own, before any training: the linear-probe head, in orange, starts at 62%, far above the prototype head, in blue, at 44%. But after the first epoch the prototype run has caught up, and from then on it stays ahead. After 3 epochs it finishes 2.5 points higher for full fine-tuning on the left, and 2.5 points higher for LoRA on the right. With LoRA the linear-probe run even stalls in the first epoch: holdout stays at 62%.
