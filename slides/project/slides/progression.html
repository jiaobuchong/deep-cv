This chart is the path to our final CodaBench score, one change per step, with the same model and 8 epochs throughout. Our full fine-tuning with the linear-probe head scored 72.57%, already above the 70.8% reference. Flip test-time augmentation added 0.27 points. Training on all 45 images per class instead of holding out 2 added another 0.26. That one is at the edge of noise, but it points the expected way. Then switching only the head initialization to prototypes added 3.12 points, to 76.22%. That last step is bigger than everything before it combined.
