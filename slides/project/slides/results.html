Here are the two final models side by side with the references, on all the metrics the brief asks for. Full fine-tuning with the prototype head, 8 epochs, all data and flip TTA reaches 76.22% on CodaBench val, 5.4 points above the reference, using 1.03 of the 1.2 allowed GPU-hours and 18.9 GB of memory. Its latency is 14.95 ms per image because of the two TTA passes; without TTA it is 7.35 ms. Our LoRA model trains only 1.375% of the backbone and reaches 71.90%, 3.6 points above the reference LoRA, in 0.54 GPU-hours. After merging, its latency equals the base model. The test-set column will be filled in after Phase 2.
